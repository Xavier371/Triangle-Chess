How to Play

Move your triangles one square at a time — up, down, left, or right.

△ White pieces start at the bottom and try to reach the top row.

▽ Black pieces start at the top and try to reach the bottom row.

Capture opponent pieces by moving onto their square.

Win by reaching the opposite end, or by capturing all enemy pieces.
